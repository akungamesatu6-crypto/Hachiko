The HACHI terms

  1. Your first share is FREE — your agent calls mintFree() on the free contract. One free share per wallet.
  2. Paid shares: your agent calls mint(uint256 shares) on the paid contract.
  3. You send 0.0015 BNB per paid share. Ten paid shares cost 0.015 BNB.
  4. You receive 1,000,000 HACHI per share — free or paid — so 10,000,000 HACHI for 10 paid shares.
  5. Your payment goes to the treasury wallet: 0x7379CA652f68fd1daAc11b7b87bb6B222fD129fa
  6. When the fund is full, the fund will be used to buy tokens with loyal communities — tokens that stay, like Hachi.
  7. There is no guaranteed price, and HACHI can be traded only if a market exists.

Quick answers

Does this site connect to my wallet?

No. This page only shows information and a prompt. Your agent makes the contract call, and you approve each step.

How much does one mint cost?

0.0015 BNB per share, plus network gas. Ten shares cost 0.015 BNB and give 10,000,000 HACHI.

Is there a guaranteed price or trading?

No. The payment goes to the treasury wallet, not into a liquidity pool, so a market price or trading is not guaranteed.

Who runs the fund?

HACHI Fund is an independent community project. The treasury wallet is 0x7379CA652f68fd1daAc11b7b87bb6B222fD129fa — every mint's payment goes there, and the fund's buys will be made from that wallet.

Will anyone ask for my seed phrase?

Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone. The prompt does not need them.

How do I get the free share?

Give the "1 free mint" prompt to your agent. It calls mintFree() on the free contract. One free share per wallet, and it gives you 1,000,000 HACHI — no BNB needed, only network gas.

Why are there two contracts?

HACHIFUND v2 splits the fund into a free pool (1,222 shares) and a paid pool (1,000 shares) so the two stay fully independent. Both mint the same HACHI token, and this page shows both pools together.
